How a job runs

Three coats of doing it right.

  1. 01

    Wash & prep

    We power-wash every surface first, then scrape, sand, and patch whatever the years have worn down. Paint only holds up as well as what's underneath it.

  2. 02

    Prime

    Bare wood, patched spots, and problem areas get primed before a drop of color goes on, so the finish coats have something solid to hold onto.

  3. 03

    Two finish coats

    Every job gets two full coats of quality paint in your chosen color, applied by a crew that cleans up after itself and leaves your home looking better than they found it.
