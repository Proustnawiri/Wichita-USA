The process

What's included in our painting services?

Every exterior painting project with HNS follows the same thorough process. We don't skip steps or cut corners, because we know that what happens before the paint goes on is just as important as the paint itself.

  1. 01

    Power-Washing

    We start every job with a full power-wash of your home's exterior. This removes dirt, mildew, chalking, and loose paint, giving us a clean surface that holds paint properly.

  2. 02

    Surface Prep & Repairs

    We inspect your siding and trim for any damage, rot, or gaps. Nail holes, cracks, and joints are caulked and filled. Any damaged siding or trim is replaced before we ever pick up a brush.

  3. 03

    Priming

    Bare wood and problem areas are primed for proper adhesion and an even finish. This step is what separates a paint job that lasts two years from one that lasts ten.

  4. 04

    Two Coats of Paint

    As a trusted Wichita painter, we value quality and affordability for our clients. We use premium exterior paint at contractor pricing and pass those savings on to you. You choose the color, we start painting.

  5. 05

    Final Walk Through

    When we're done, we walk the job with you. If anything needs a touch-up or an extra bit of attention, we take care of it before we call the job complete.
