Rooms & Cabinets Refreshed, Start to Finish

The inside of your home deserves just as much attention as the outside. Our interior painting services cover detailing walls, trim, and cabinetry — the surfaces you actually live with every day, and the ones that show wear first.

The process

What's included in our interior services?

Every interior job with HNS follows the same careful process, whether it's a single accent wall or a full-home repaint.

  1. 01

    Protection & Setup

    Floors, furniture, and fixtures are covered before anything else happens. Edges, trim, and hardware are taped off so lines stay clean and your home stays tidy through the whole job.

  2. 02

    Wall & Trim Repair

    Nail holes, dings, and cracked drywall are patched and sanded smooth. Trim and cabinet gaps are caulked so the finished surface looks seamless, not painted-over.

  3. 03

    Priming

    Patched areas, stains, and bare wood get spot-primed — or fully primed on cabinetry — so your finish coats go on even and hold their color for years, not months.

  4. 04

    Two Coats of Paint

    We use quality interior paint at contractor pricing and pass the savings on to you. Walls, trim, and cabinetry each get two full coats in the color you choose.

  5. 05

    Final Walk Through

    We walk every room with you before we call it done. Any touch-ups get handled on the spot, and we leave your space as clean as we found it.
